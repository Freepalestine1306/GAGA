Mbappe



Kylian Mbappé is a French professional footballer who plays as a forward for Real Madrid and the France national team.

A prolific scorer, he became France's all-time top scorer and led the 2025/26 UEFA Champions League with 15 goals.

He has won multiple Ligue 1 titles, French Cups, and reached a Champions League final with Paris Saint-Germain

before joining Real Madrid, amassing over 400 career goals in club and international play.
