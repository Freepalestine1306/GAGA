Cristiano Ronaldo

41 years • Feb 5, 1985

Cristiano Ronaldo, nicknamed CR7, is a Portuguese professional footballer who plays as a forward for Al-Nassr and captains Portugal. Widely regarded as one of the greatest of all time, he has won five Ballons d'Or, five UEFA Champions Leagues, Euro 2016, and two UEFA Nations Leagues. Ronaldo holds records for most international goals (146) and appearances (234), and has scored over 980 senior career goals.
